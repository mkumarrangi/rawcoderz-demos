## Current targeting focus

Prioritize established staffed cafes, restaurants and local chains with a clear ordering need and willingness to invest. Very small self-operated venues are lower priority for this campaign. Business size, review count and menu prices alone do not establish a budget.

Start by qualifying Cafe LazyMojo, On The House, Tapri, Curious Life, Anokhi Cafe, Niros and Spice Court. These are research candidates, not confirmed buyers. Keep larger groups as strategic accounts, and confirm a technology gap where an ordering system already exists.

See [targeting strategy and all 28 priority assignments](targeting-strategy.md). Each detailed record now includes its provisional outreach priority.

## Outreach preparation

Use the detailed record to select one outlet, confirm the manager or operations contact, and ask about the existing order flow before building a personalized demo. Public emails are research leads, not confirmed active inboxes. General, customer-service and press inboxes may need to redirect the enquiry.

The demo ideas are proposals based on menu and venue type. They do not establish that a business currently has queues or service problems. Any kitchen gallery, menu prices, dietary claims or preparation estimates should be approved by that business.
