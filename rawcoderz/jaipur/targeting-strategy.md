# Jaipur Client Targeting Strategy

Updated: 11 October 2026

## Campaign direction

Focus demo work on established cafes, restaurants and local food chains that have staff, enough order volume to benefit from better ordering, and willingness to invest. Very small self-operated shops are lower priority under the user's campaign preference.

This is a sales focus, not a claim that every small business refuses technology. A larger venue can also have no available budget or already have a suitable system. No prospect's revenue, staffing, budget or purchase intent has been verified.

## Qualify before a full personalized demo

- **Operating scale:** confirm regular service staff, seating or meaningful takeaway volume; multiple outlets are a useful signal.
- **An actual need:** ask how dine-in and pickup orders are handled and identify a specific issue they want to improve.
- **A decision-maker:** reach the owner, manager or operations team that can approve the work.
- **Ability and willingness to invest:** confirm a budget range after Rawcoderz defines its offer and pricing.
- **Operational fit:** identify who will maintain the menu and how orders will reach the kitchen or existing POS.

Build the full personalized demo once these points are sufficiently clear. Budget, staffing and an ordering gap should not be inferred from review counts, attractive interiors or an absent website button.

## Email requirement for outreach

The user's campaign requires a usable business email for each email-outreach candidate. See the [email contact register](email-contacts.md). Record an address only when a public source can be matched to the intended business. Prefer the business's own website; confirm listing-only contacts before relying on them. Keep venues with missing emails pending for email outreach and request their preferred inbox during qualification. No address has been delivery-tested, and no outreach has been sent.

## Position the offer around the business need

Lead with a visual menu, easier ordering, accurate customizations, pickup convenience and a clear kitchen handoff. Explain how the proposed flow fits their staff and current system. A kitchen or preparation gallery can support the brand story if the business supplies approved material.

For venues with existing websites or apps, demonstrate the specific improvement agreed during discovery. Avoid promising zero queues, increased sales or reduced staffing without evidence from a pilot.

Keep Kanha and Tansukh within one coordinated group conversation. For larger organisations, identify the operations or digital contact and scope a single-location pilot before preparing a multi-outlet proposal.
