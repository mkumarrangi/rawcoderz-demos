Our specialty

Trusted by music stores, professional musicians and teachers

Some pieces are more than furniture. Pianos, organs and clocks carry weight, history and delicate mechanisms, and they’re what we’re known for.

i

Spinets & uprights

Compact but heavy, top-loaded and tricky on stairs. Padded, strapped and rolled with care.

ii

Grand pianos

Legs and lyre removed, the case wrapped and secured to a piano board, then reassembled on arrival.

iii

Pipe organs

Large, intricate instruments that call for planning, the right equipment and a steady crew.

iv

Grandfather clocks

Weights, pendulum and glass secured separately so the movement arrives safe and ready to run.
