White-glove care

Wrapped in the room, before anything moves.

Every piece is protected where it stands. Here is the standard on every Golden Movers job.

  1. Full pad wrap, in the roomFurniture is wrapped where it stands, before it ever moves.
  2. Shrink wrap on upholsterySofas and upholstered chairs sealed against dust and scuffs.
  3. Glass padded & cardedGlass pieces padded and secured with cardboard.
  4. Mattresses baggedEvery mattress travels clean in its own bag.
  5. Disassembly & reassemblyBeds and furniture taken apart and put back together.
  6. Rug runners in walkwaysYour floors are covered along every path the crew walks.
